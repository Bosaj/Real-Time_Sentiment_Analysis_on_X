# Observability & Quality Assurance: Real-Time_Sentiment_Analysis_on_X

This repository incorporates the 4-Pillar Observability and QA Suite:
1. **JSON Logging**: `monitoring/logger.py` / `logger.js`
2. **Prometheus & Grafana**: `monitoring/prometheus/prometheus.yml` & `monitoring/grafana/dashboard.json`
3. **Health Endpoints**: Liveness probe & readiness probe in `monitoring/health.py` / `health.js`
4. **Evaluation Harness**: Accuracy & latency evaluation in `scripts/eval_harness.py`
